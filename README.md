## Overview
This project was proposed by Caleb Rezmer for the FSAE club at Iowa State. This converter will go inside the electric formula car and it will use the 600 Volt battery from the car and step it down to 12 Volts at 30 Amps. It is stepped down to 12 Volts for the internals of the car such as lights, digital displays, fans, and anything else the team would want to add to the car that requires low voltage.

## Role
My role in the project was to make sure the power supply had the proper components it needs to function properly. I had to build and edit the schematic from the reference design we had. I had to verify that at the proper test points it was able to take that high voltage and keep the voltage at a steady 12 volts.
